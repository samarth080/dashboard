do not infer demographics, private facts, personality diagnoses, or experiences
that are not present in the text.

- a short evidence-based summary;
- tone descriptors;
- recurring sentence patterns;
- vocabulary preferences;
- phrases or habits to avoid when matching this voice;
- formatting preferences;
- signature traits; and
- a confidence score from 0 to 1 that reflects sample quantity and consistency.

Use empty lists when the samples do not support a category. Do not invent
examples or quote more text than necessary.
