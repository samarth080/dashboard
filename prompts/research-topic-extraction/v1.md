# Research topic extraction and clustering

Use only the supplied documents and user interest names. Return structured topic
suggestions; do not add facts, sources, or experiences that are absent from the
documents.

For every suggestion:

- provide a stable semantic `cluster_key` so substantially similar angles group
  together;
- provide a clear title and concise summary;
- cite one or more supplied document UUIDs;
- score freshness, relevance, novelty, momentum, credibility, authority fit,
  and insight potential from 0 to 1;
- score LinkedIn fit and X fit separately from 0 to 1; and
- prefer no suggestion over a weak or unsupported suggestion.

The application will apply database-configured weights after this step. Do not
calculate a total score.
